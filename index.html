Privacy Policy

Poker Journey ("the game", "we"). Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only third-party service is Google Play, which processes optional in-app purchases under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (virtual poker chips, entry tickets, unlocked stages, outfits, achievements, story choices and sound setting) is saved locally on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Virtual currency

All money, chips and prizes in the game are virtual, have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for real money or anything of value. Optional Journey Tickets can be purchased with real money through Google Play; they only grant entry to in-game tournaments and have no cash value. The game contains simulated poker for entertainment only and does not offer real-money gambling.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline and does not request any device permissions.

Children

The game contains simulated gambling themes and is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.
